LossPacket brand guidelines

LossPacket · Paper and Ink · Joined LP · Version 1.0 · 14 October 2026.

This system turns the selected editorial case-file direction into editable identity assets, semantic tokens, reusable controls, original visual material and 33 animated page studies. It preserves the brief’s five independent offers and research-stage status. The kit is ready to guide a website build; it is not an operating claims product.

Identity

The Joined LP pairs a legible L foot with an open P counter. The symbol and wordmark are native paths, not an image embedded in an SVG. The selected Joined LP symbol is redrawn with bespoke Bezier paths from the chosen reference: curved L foot, open lower P bowl and a plain P stem. All lockups and favicons share this master geometry. The wordmark uses Source Serif 4 outlines.

Use ink on paper as the primary identity, cream on ink for reverse, and the supplied true black and slate variants when required. Keep at least one P-stem width of clear space on every side. Use the horizontal lockup in navigation and packet headers, the stacked lockup in social or compact editorial layouts, and the standalone symbol for app/folder usage.

Recommended minimums: 160px wide for the horizontal lockup, 32px for the normal symbol. For small applications use the supplied padded LP symbol and favicon exports; the selected geometry is preserved. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, enclose in an approval seal, animate the letters apart or introduce warning amber to the logo. Maintain an accessible text name where the logo is a link.

Included: 16 primary/reverse/slate/black SVG variants; an optical small symbol; transparent PNG exports; SVG/adaptive SVG favicon; ICO with multiple actual sizes; 16/32/48/64/96/180/192/512 PNGs; touch icon and manifest. The favicon background is a distinct ink app tile, not a security shield.

Palette and surfaces

Preserved anchors: paper #F6F2EA, ink #1B2230, parchment #E7E1D5, slate #2F4B6E and warning amber #C98A2B. Derived roles add an almost-white reading surface, fog working surface, night ink, slate hover/pressed tones and contrasting warning/error/review text. Semantic tokens keep role names stable across light and dark themes.

Background rhythm: paper reading chapter → ink source/review chapter → parchment working chapter → clear paper form. Every large change marks a new narrative task. Never make alternating colors the only distinction between sections.

Use amber only to indicate missing or held evidence. Pair warning color with a textual reason and appropriate icon. Use the darker amber-text token on the pale warning surface; the raw brand amber is not approved for ordinary small text on cream. “Review recorded” green labels refer to a review event, not insurer approval.

All 22 specified text/action/focus/control pairings passed their stated ratio threshold in the calculated audit. This is a color-pair check, not a blanket WCAG certification of a future website. Measured audit.

Typography and text

Self-host Source Serif 4 normal and italic for editorial headings and selected source excerpts. IBM Plex Sans normal and italic supplies body, navigation, forms and help text. IBM Plex Mono regular/medium/italic supplies case IDs, dates, page references and tabular financial figures. OFL licenses ship with the files. Font declarations use swap and complete fallbacks.

Layout and shapes

Use a 4px base with the 4/8/12/16/24/32/40/48/64/80/96/128/160 scale. Main content is 1200px, wide material scenes up to 1440px, reading columns 680px. Desktop grid: 12 columns and 24px gaps; tablet: 8 columns; mobile: 4 columns with 16px gaps and 24px gutters. Typical chapters use 96–128px desktop and 64px mobile vertical space.

Shape families: squared 2px paper documents; 4px fields; 6px buttons; 8px workspace cards; a single 24px outer hero frame; restrained arched framing reserved for owner/company visual chapters. A clipped index tab and margin rail can replace a rounded box. Give the five products visibly distinct artifacts, not five copies of one decorative card.

Buttons and hover system

Primary controls are 48px tall, with at least 44px targets for prominent actions. The seven implemented hover families are shutter fill, identical-label flip, arrow/icon crossfade, directional nudge, rule draw, index-tab reveal and solid-color wipe. Cards add folio-tab extension, margin tint, source underline/highlight and inset border reveal. Every relevant hover has keyboard-focus parity.

Use shutter for primary navigation/pilot actions, label flip for one secondary promotional action per section, underline for editorial links and source highlighting for source references. Keep the action wording stable: an arrow change does not create a new action. All essential content remains visible before hover. Disable effects on disabled controls. Fields include default, focus, invalid, disabled and help states; the kit’s email fields never submit or save data.

Timing: 140ms micro, 180–260ms state, 320ms card, 600ms section. Standard easing is cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Avoid large tilts, magnetic cursors, cursor-dependent content, elastic movement and repeated bouncing. CSS transitions and MDN performance guidance inform using transform and opacity for appropriate motion; decorative color changes may repaint small controls.

Material and photography

Six original image assets cover an upright folio, policy margins, incident contact sheets, an income ledger, an ink review desk and paired review places. They are generated decorative props, not genuine insurance documents or evidence photographs. No customer, insurer or financial-system marks appear. Keep real headings, source IDs and product controls outside photographs.

Eight editable SVG textures cover paper grain, ledger rules, margin grid, binding lines, registration crosses, source dots, folio weave and ink grain. Use texture at very low contrast on margins; reading panels remain flat. Crop photo scenes by their supplied negative space rather than placing prose over source details. Reuse the material family across pages but change composition and focus.

Icons and pictograms

The kit includes 41 functional Lucide SVGs and two social SVGs, plus 20 original 64px brand pictograms. Functional strokes follow the 24px Lucide system; large brand pictograms use a coherent 64px grid, 1.75px strokes and round line joins. Symbol captions carry role meaning. Do not use courthouse, shield or approval-seal imagery to describe preparation.

LinkedIn’s icon geometry was extracted unchanged from the official sprite linked on its brand downloads page, with the official PNG originals retained. Crunchbase’s icon is sourced from Simple Icons. Brand marks are used for identification, not endorsement; retain their geometry. Footer buttons are specimens until actual company profile URLs are verified. Lucide license is ISC with additional MIT terms for Feather-derived icons; both notices are included. Social-mark usage conditions remain separate from artwork licenses.

Motion grammar

Hero art settles once with a short material shift; documents enter in source order; connectors draw between explicit source ports; a working paper reveals its inputs and assumptions; a route pauses at a review hold. All sequences are finite, normally 4.2 seconds, and can be replayed. Global Pause and Reduce motion controls are functional. The system respects prefers-reduced-motion, suspends offscreen sequences and stops motion while the tab is hidden.

Families include animated synthetic premises maps, evidence/provenance networks, reviewed process diagrams, source-linked financial displays, numbered steps, professional-role figures, document and version line illustrations and registration patterns. The kit uses coded SVG/CSS animation, not MP4 exports; these are the chosen implementation-ready hero treatment. No autoplay movement suggests an insurer determination.

A complete static frame and textual explanation remain available. The mobile diagram may scroll inside its own panel to keep source labels readable; its purpose and important case facts are repeated as ordinary text. W3C pause/stop guidance informs controls for longer parallel movement; reduced-motion guidance informs the still-state fallback. Do not claim complete accessibility conformance solely from these mechanisms.

The additional motion library includes source-registration patterns, institutional/professional-role pictograms and technology/provenance line studies. Four editable composition frames—indexed sheet, margin bracket, soft arch and ledger band—ship in assets/shapes.

Brief and copy guardrails

Keep all five offers independent and labeled proposed modules. Their scopes remain: policy/endorsement coverage-question inventory; incident timeline/photo/invoice organizer; business-income loss working papers linked to source books; insurer request/deadline tracking and approved response drafts; settlement reconciliation and missing-item appeal packet for professional review. The Claims Report preview keeps settlement input unprovided for the canonical CL-14 path.

Use Alder Oven CL-14, loss 12 February 2026; PH-01/R-09/P-08; R-09 $6,000; P-08 $4,800; prior-period revenue $28,000; incident revenue $4,000; illustrative difference $24,000. This is not a payable-loss formula. The suspension end remains disputed between 22 and 25 February, and reopening evidence is missing. Keep insurer determination blank and sending unavailable.

Do not reproduce incidental generated text, dates or calculations from the visual-direction screenshot. The IA and brief control facts. No testimonials, customer logos, recovered dollars, production integrations, certifications or causal payout claims. Hypothetical pilot prices stay explicitly hypothetical. Contact, updates and claim-material consent are separate purposes.

Implementation handoff

Open index.html directly for a portable specimen, or serve the folder with a static server. Fonts, SVGs, photos and page-scene data are local; the kit has no third-party runtime scripts. Import tokens/fonts.css, tokens/tokens.css and tokens/components.css into the eventual website. Use page-hero-registry.json to assign the correct source scene and composition.

Do not copy the entire specimen into a public website. Its downloads, builder notes and preview-state messages belong to the kit. Replace them with the IA’s complete buyer pages and consented live intake only after the required operator, licensing, handling and pilot gates are resolved.
